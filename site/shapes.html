SCHEMA.md 5 / derived from bytes

Twenty-four labels. Eighteen have a capture.

A label is not a tag somebody typed into a sidecar. It is a rule validate.py applies to account.json, and the sidecar has to agree with the bytes or the corpus fails. So this page is two views of one derived set: the grid, for seeing a fixture across the vocabulary, and the list, for picking the fixture that has the shape a rule needs.

Six labels, no capture

What the search saw, and the task that owns each hole.

The uncovered vocabulary
Label What it takes Owner
no_signers An account resource whose signers array is empty. The only reads that would show it here are ones nobody has found yet, and a shape this thin is exactly where a hand-typed fixture would hide a wrong assumption. roadmap 2.4, issue #14
master_absent Horizon omitting the master key from signers, which is what a revoked master looks like on the account resource. Rare, and one of the accounts known to have this shape is unrecoverable, so it may not be invented. roadmap 2.4, issue #14
auth_revocable Three issuer accounts, one flag set each. The issuers found while looking carried auth_revocable and auth_clawback_enabled on the same account, and one issuer with all three flags would test one account rather than three labels. roadmap 2.5, issue #15
auth_immutable roadmap 2.5, issue #15
auth_clawback_enabled roadmap 2.5, issue #15
no_balances An account that has never held a trustline. No account read for this corpus, on either network, has shown an empty balances array: every one carries at least its native line. This row may end in a decision that the label stays uncovered and a reason recorded, rather than in a capture. roadmap 2.3, issue #13

Synthetic is allowed and unused

The schema permits a fixture authored rather than read, on two conditions: it carries none of the five provenance keys, because it has no URL, no ledger and no digest to name, and its scenario starts with synthetic:. No synthetic fixture is shipped here, so every number on this page and on the overview describes a real read. Closing a gap with a synthetic capture is a decision to record in README.md and the roadmap, not a way to make the list above shorter.
